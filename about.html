About Me

Software Developer | Cloud Security | AI

My Path

My tech journey really started at community college. I worked in IT for the Yosemite Community College District for about two and a half years, which gave me my first real exposure to security, networking, and day-to-day troubleshooting outside the classroom.

After transferring to CSU Stanislaus, I joined Cal-Bridge and started getting involved in research. The biggest project from that period was a summer internship at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, where I worked on computer vision for autonomous drones and trained a YOLOv5 model for real-time object detection.

I continued into an MS in Computer Science at UC Davis, where I focused more on networking and security. I also TA'd every quarter, covering courses from assembly and system architecture to data structures and algorithms.

After graduating, I spent time building projects and sharpening my cloud security skills through CompTIA Security+, Terraform, and Azure certifications. Two projects I focused on most were an Azure honeypot that captures RDP brute-force attempts and maps attacker origins, and FormFixAI, an Azure app that uses Google Gemini 2.5 to analyze workout videos and give form feedback.

Right now I'm a Systems & Programming Analyst at YCCD on contract through August. In September 2026, I'm joining IBM as a Software Developer.
